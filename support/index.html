YouTube Live

In VolleyCam, open Settings → YouTube Live Streaming. You can sign out, disconnect authorization, manage Manual RTMPS, and open the public privacy policy there.

Protect sensitive information

PAL Studio support will never ask for your Google password, passkey, two-factor code, OAuth token, or YouTube stream key. Enter those only in the appropriate Google or device interface.
