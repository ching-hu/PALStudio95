VolleyCam Privacy Policy

Effective August 25, 2026 · Last updated September 14, 2026

Privacy summary. VolleyCam does not operate an advertising, analytics, account, or media server and does not sell or track user data. Core match information, media, and settings remain on the user’s devices. Optional YouTube Live sends user-authorized account requests and user-selected live content directly to Google/YouTube to provide that feature.

Privacy at a Glance

VolleyCam does not require an account for core recording, Camera/Remote, scorekeeping, rally, match-browser, or export features. PAL Studio does not collect those users’ match data or media on developer-operated servers. VolleyCam does not include advertising, tracking, third-party analytics, or third-party crash-reporting services.

Information Stored on the Device

VolleyCam may store settings, team and tournament names, scores, set information, match timelines, thumbnails, Photos references, Camera/Remote pairing information, and other app state locally. Recordings and exported clips are stored on the device and, when the user chooses, in Apple Photos.

Camera and Remote devices exchange encrypted control messages, scores, match metadata, connection state, and low-resolution previews over the local network. This information is not routed through a PAL Studio server.

Device Permissions and Optional Services

Camera and microphone permission enables match video and audio. Photos permission supports saving, previewing, organizing, trimming, and updating a selected clip’s Favorite status. Motion permission detects a meaningful tripod-framing change. Optional location permission can add an approximate recording location to saved-video metadata. Local Network permission enables nearby Camera/Remote operation.

Optional tips are processed by Apple through StoreKit. VolleyCam does not receive payment-card information or retain raw StoreKit receipts on a PAL Studio server.

Google and YouTube User Data

YouTube Live Streaming is optional. VolleyCam requests only https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.force-ssl. This scope is necessary because read-only access cannot create, bind, update, start, or complete the user’s live-broadcast resources. VolleyCam does not request Gmail, Drive, Contacts, Calendar, YouTube Analytics, or YouTube Partner access, and it never asks for or receives a Google password.

1. What Google and YouTube user data VolleyCam accesses

After the user chooses YouTube Live Streaming and authorizes access, VolleyCam processes:

  • Google/YouTube authorization credentials managed by the official Google Sign-In SDK, including an access token used for YouTube API requests and authorization state used to restore the connection;
  • the selected YouTube channel ID, channel title, and channel-thumbnail URL;
  • broadcast title, description, scheduled time, privacy setting, audience setting, thumbnail, lifecycle status, broadcast ID, bound live-stream ID, and viewer/watch URL;
  • live-stream ID, status, compatible resolution/frame rate, secure RTMPS ingestion address, and ingestion stream name required for the active session; and
  • camera video, microphone audio, and visible scoreboard content only after the user explicitly starts a livestream.

2. How VolleyCam uses Google and YouTube user data

VolleyCam uses this data only to provide the user-requested YouTube Live feature: identify the signed-in channel; restore the authorized connection; list, create, select, update, and bind broadcasts and live streams; start, monitor, recover, and complete a broadcast; publish the selected live feed; obtain the viewer link; and let the user open, copy, display as a QR code, or share that link.

Google user data is not used for advertising, user profiling, data brokerage, unrelated analytics, or training generalized artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models.

3. With whom Google and YouTube user data is shared or transferred

VolleyCam does not sell Google user data and does not share it with advertisers or data brokers. Authorization, channel, broadcast, and stream requests travel directly between the user’s device and Google/YouTube as needed to perform the requested action. Live video, audio, and scoreboard content travel directly from the user’s device to YouTube during a livestream.

A public viewer URL and safe broadcast status/title may be sent to the user’s paired VolleyCam Remote device so that the user can use sharing controls there. The viewer URL reaches other people only when the user explicitly invokes iOS sharing, copies or opens it, or presents its QR code. OAuth credentials, authorization headers, RTMPS ingestion addresses, and stream names/keys are not included in Camera/Remote messages, QR codes, or app-generated share and clipboard payloads.

4. How sensitive Google and YouTube user data is protected

VolleyCam uses TLS/HTTPS for Google OAuth and YouTube API requests and requires RTMPS for live media. The official Google Sign-In SDK manages persisted Google authorization and token refresh using Google and iOS platform security. A manually entered RTMPS key is stored separately in the iOS Keychain with device-only protection.

OAuth tokens, authorization headers, RTMPS credentials, and manual stream keys are excluded from match/rally metadata, ordinary logs, Remote messages, QR codes, and app-generated sharing. Error logs use categories and numeric diagnostics rather than response bodies or credentials.

5. How Google and YouTube user data is retained and deleted

The Google Sign-In SDK retains authorization state locally so the user can remain signed in. VolleyCam caches channel ID, title, thumbnail URL, and validation time locally for up to 24 hours. It may retain non-secret broadcast/stream IDs, title, selected quality, and viewer URL while needed to restore or manage a prepared or interrupted session. Reusable-stream configuration metadata may be cached to avoid creating duplicates. Runtime RTMPS ingestion credentials remain only in memory while preparing or running the active session and are then cleared.

Sign Out in Settings → YouTube Live Streaming ends the local Google session and clears VolleyCam’s channel and account-derived session/resource caches, but does not revoke the Google grant. Disconnect YouTube Account asks Google to revoke VolleyCam authorization and clears those local caches. The user may also revoke VolleyCam through Google Account connections. Forget Stream Key separately deletes a manually saved RTMPS key; other Manual RTMPS preferences remain independent of Google account disconnect.

Sign out, disconnect, and app deletion do not delete videos, broadcasts, or reusable stream resources already in the user’s YouTube account. The user controls them in YouTube Studio. Local VolleyCam matches and videos are independent and remain until separately deleted in VolleyCam, Photos, or iOS.

PAL Studio does not retain Google user data on developer-operated servers.

Other Retention and Deletion

Local app data remains until the user deletes it with VolleyCam controls, removes the app, erases the device, or otherwise manages it through iOS. Media saved to Photos can remain after VolleyCam is removed and must be deleted in Photos. PAL Studio cannot access, recover, or delete content stored only on the user’s devices or personal Photos library.

Children’s Privacy

VolleyCam does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults on PAL Studio servers. Parents, guardians, coaches, schools, clubs, and event organizers are responsible for obtaining permissions required before recording or sharing images or videos of minors. The user explicitly selects YouTube’s audience classification; VolleyCam does not infer it from player age.

Third-Party Terms and International Processing

Apple system services and StoreKit operate under Apple’s privacy practices. When YouTube Live is used, Google may process authorized account and broadcast information in countries where Google operates under the Google Privacy Policy, Google API Services User Data Policy, YouTube Terms of Service, and the user’s settings.

Changes and Contact
